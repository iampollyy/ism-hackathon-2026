# Demo guide

How to show Callback live, on https://callback.denishoti.dev. You need a laptop and two phones, all online.

## Before the demo (about 10 minutes, once)

1. **Laptop:** Register, then "Create a family plan". Add yourself as the organiser, tick both of your own boxes (practice calls, and "Callback may copy my voice from my welcome call"), and add two people, for example Mum and Jonas. Choose "Start the 30-day free trial".
2. **Each phone:** open that person's invite (Copy, Share, or scan the QR code on the invite step, or later with "Add a person" on the family page). Tick both boxes and tap "Join and start my welcome call".
   - The welcome call rings by itself. Answer, say yes to both questions, and answer the questions in a few sentences. That copies the voice after a spoken yes (about 30 seconds of speech is enough).
   - Optional: "Get call alerts", and "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone) or "Install Callback" (Android).
3. **Laptop, family page:** switch **Protection** on and check that the phones show the green "phone open" dot.
4. Keep both phone pages open, with the screen on and the sound up. Tap "Tap to switch on sound" once on each.

## Demo 1: a practice drill (about 60 seconds)

1. Laptop, family page: **Surprise drill** for Mum (or "Start a practice": who, the story, whose voice, now). Click "Watch it live" to open the live call screen.
2. Mum's phone rings: "Unknown number". She answers. The caller speaks in Jonas's cloned voice: a new number, urgency, a request for money today.
3. The laptop shows the conversation line by line, a tactic label as each tactic is used, and the traffic light turning yellow, then red.
4. Mum asks for the family word, or says she'll hang up and call back. The caller stops at once and reveals that it was a practice, with a one-line lesson.
5. The laptop shows what she did and how many seconds it took her to challenge the caller. The Drills page keeps the replay.

## Demo 2: Callback Shield (about 40 seconds)

The Shield is the protection mode: it warns the person during a scam call and alerts the family by itself.

1. Laptop, family page, "Show it working": **Shield demo call** for Mum. The laptop opens the live call screen.
2. Mum answers and plays along: "Oh no, what happened?"
3. The caller claims to be the bank and asks her to move money today. In our test on the live site, **about 2 seconds** after that line:
   - **Mum's phone** shows a full-screen red warning: "This sounds like a scam. Hang up and call Ana on their usual number", with Hang up and Call buttons, and the phone vibrates where it can.
   - **Jonas's phone** (and the organiser's) gets the alert: "Shield demo, nothing is wrong: Mum is on a practice scam call."
   - **The laptop** shows "Shield alerted the family".
4. Mum taps Hang up. The call ends with the reveal.

Say it plainly: this weekend the call comes through the app; in the product, real calls are forwarded to a Callback line, and the same check runs on them.

## If something doesn't ring

- **"Open Mum's phone link first":** the phone page isn't open, or the screen locked. Open it again; the green dot comes back within a second.
- **Nothing rings right after a call:** a new drill waits 2 minutes after the person's last call, so it never cuts into a reveal.
- **No sound on the phone:** tap "Tap to switch on sound", check the volume, and on an iPhone use iOS 16.4 or later.
- **The organiser shows "not agreed":** families made before 14 Nov morning had no voice tick for the organiser. Open your own phone link ("Join on this phone") once and tick both boxes.
- **Daily limits** (to protect the ElevenLabs credit): 30 new families, 25 voice copies and 150 calls a day on the live site.
