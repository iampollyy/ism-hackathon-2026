# Callback video kit

Everything lives in `video/` at the repo root.

1. Read `video/CLAUDE.md` first: folder layout, workflow, what makes it look real, and the rules (consent, AI labels, wording, never committing footage or keys).
2. Commands and the edit-list format are in `video/README.md`.
3. If `video/tools/.venv` or `video/remotion/node_modules` is missing, ask the person to run `bash video/setup.sh` once (it also installs the HeyGen, Remotion and ElevenLabs skills).
4. Brand colours, fonts and logos: `brand/`.

A person watches every cut on a phone before it is posted. Say so when you hand one over.
